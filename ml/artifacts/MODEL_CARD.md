# Personalized Heat Risk Demonstrator — Model Card

- **Model:** standardized logistic regression
- **Training records:** 12,000 synthetic records generated by `train_personalized_risk.py`
- **Target:** synthetic risk label generated from a heuristic; it is not a real clinical outcome
- **Holdout ROC AUC (synthetic only):** 0.729
- **Holdout accuracy (synthetic only):** 0.665
- **Limitations:** no clinical validation, no external validation, no calibration for real populations, and no basis for clinical decisions.
- **Next step:** replace synthetic labels with ethically approved, consented and independently reviewed data; evaluate by subgroup and geography before any real-world use.
